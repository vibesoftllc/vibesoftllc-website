Dotmates

Privacy notice  ·  Effective September 1, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Dotmates collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but here is what it means specifically.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app.

What Dotmates stores

Your progress — which levels you have solved and the stars you have earned — and your preferences for music, appearance, Expert Mode and shape cues. These are kept on your device using the system's standard app preferences. They never leave your device except through your own device backups, which you control.

What Dotmates does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no email is collected.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store's sense or any other.
  • No in-app purchases. The game is free and complete.

Network access

Dotmates makes no network requests. It contains no networking code.

The one place a link appears is the "More from Vibesoft" screen, which opens a page on vibesoftllc.com in your browser. That is your device opening a link; Dotmates itself sends nothing, and shares nothing about you.

Children

Dotmates collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves the device.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.

Questions about this app: dev@vibesoftllc.com
