Deedbox

Privacy notice  ·  Effective September 21, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Deedbox collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but here is what it means specifically.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline — the app renders this exact file rather than a second copy that could drift out of step with it.

What Deedbox stores, and where

Your properties, the rent you have received and the expenses you have recorded are stored on your device. If you have iCloud turned on, they also sync to your own private iCloud database — an area of your Apple account that we cannot read, list, or access in any way.

We operate no server for Deedbox. There is nowhere for your rent roll to be sent, because there is nothing on the other end. We do not know how many properties you have, what they earn, or that you opened the app.

That includes anything you record about a tenant — a name, an email address, a phone number, the terms of a lease. Those are your records like any other: kept on your device and in your own iCloud, never sent to us, and never used by Deedbox to contact anyone.

Why this matters more than usual here

The IRS can look back seven years. Most rental bookkeeping is a subscription to somebody's servers, which means a seven-year record depends on that company still existing, still charging what it charges today, and still including export in the tier you pay for.

Deedbox has no server to lose. Your records are files on your own device, and the year-end export is never behind the purchase — the way out is not the thing being sold.

What Deedbox does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no email is collected.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Deedbox links no third-party code at all.
  • No tracking, in the App Store's sense or any other.
  • No camera or photo-library access. This version does not photograph or read receipts, and does not ask for permission it would not use.

Network access

Two things involve the network, and neither is Deedbox sending your data to us:

  • iCloud sync, if you have it on, moves your records between your own devices through your own Apple account. Apple handles it; we have no visibility into it.
  • Purchasing the unlock goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, only Apple's aggregate sales reporting.

More from Vibesoft

Settings → About has a More from Vibesoft screen that lists our other apps. Tapping a row opens that app's App Store page, or our website for CruiseSort, in the App Store or your browser. That is your device opening a page when you ask it to, not Deedbox sending anything: the list is built into the app, Deedbox does not know which row you tapped, keeps no record of it, and has nowhere to send one.

The free-tier counter

Deedbox remembers how many properties you have created so that the one-property free tier works as intended. That count is a single number kept in your device's keychain, which syncs through your own Apple account. It is not sent to us and we cannot read it. No other information travels with it.

Your exports

The year-end CSV and PDF are generated on your device. Where they go afterwards is up to you — the share sheet hands them to whatever app you pick, and from that point they are between you and that app. Worth remembering that those documents contain your financial records.

Not tax advice

Deedbox organises records into the shape of IRS Schedule E (Form 1040) Part I. It does not prepare or file a return, it does not compute figures it lacks the information to compute, and nothing in it has been reviewed by a tax professional. Every document it exports says so on the page. Consult a qualified preparer.

Children

Deedbox collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves the device.

The company policy
