Hunt Advisor

Privacy notice  ·  Effective October 28, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Hunt Advisor collects nothing. It is a local-first app: no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, under ⚙ About, readable there offline.

What the app collects

Nothing. No personal data is collected, stored off your device, sold, or shared. There is no Vibesoft server to send it to.

Location

If you grant location permission, your position is shown on the hunt chart and stamped onto finds you choose to log. Location is processed entirely on your device and never transmitted. Your finds log stays on the device; it leaves only as text you export and share yourself.

Network requests

The app fetches public, anonymous ocean and weather data directly from government and public services:

  • NOAA NDBC buoys and NOAA CO-OPS tide stations
  • The National Weather Service
  • NOAA model data served by PacIOOS (University of Hawaiʻi) and NOAA CoastWatch
  • CDIP wave buoys
  • Map tiles from USGS The National Map

These are ordinary anonymous requests for public data; no account, identifier, or personal information goes with them. Fetched reports are cached on your device so the app works offline.

Purchases

Purchases are one-time, never subscriptions, and handled entirely by Apple. The app only asks Apple’s StoreKit, on your device, which items you own. Vibesoft never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. The home coast you pick is saved in your iCloud Keychain so it follows you to a new phone; it is encrypted by Apple and not readable by Vibesoft.

Notifications

Optional GO alerts are computed and posted locally on your device. Turning them on sends nothing anywhere.

More from Vibesoft

The About screen lists other Vibesoft apps as plain links. The list is built into the app, nothing is fetched to show it, and nothing records what you tap.

Data retention & deletion

Everything the app stores — cached reports, preferences, your finds log, alert state — lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Export your finds first if you want to keep them.

Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy will appear in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
