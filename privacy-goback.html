Go Back?

Privacy notice  ·  Effective October 28, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Your ratings are private, and Go Back? collects nothing. There is no account, no public profile, no followers and no review that anyone else can read.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page says the same thing as the policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline.

Where your places live

Every place you rate, every visit, every verdict and every note is stored on your device — and so is every recommendation you save, including the name you give for whoever made it. If iCloud is turned on for Go Back?, the same data syncs through your own private iCloud database to your other devices. Apple holds that copy under your Apple Account. Vibesoft cannot see it.

We operate no server. Your ratings are never sent to us, because there is nowhere for them to be sent.

What Apple Maps sees

When you search for a place, tap a place on the map, or open the rating screen to see places nearby, Go Back? uses Apple Maps search. The text you type and the area you are searching in go to Apple, as they do with any map search on your device. Your ratings and notes are never part of a search. Apple’s handling of map searches is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Location

Go Back? asks for your location only while you are using it. It uses your location to show nearby places, to measure how far away your rated places are, and to pin a place you add by name. A location is stored only as the position of a place you chose to save. Go Back? never tracks you in the background.

What Go Back? does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store’s sense or any other.
  • No subscription, so there is no billing relationship and no renewal to lapse.

Network access

Go Back? makes no requests to any server of ours, because there is none. Three things use the network, and none of them is your data going to us:

  • Apple Maps search, as described above.
  • iCloud sync, between your own devices, through Apple.
  • Buying the unlock, through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Separately, More from Vibesoft (Settings › About) lists our other apps. It is built into the app and fetched from nowhere. Tapping a row opens that app’s App Store page, or our website for CruiseSort, in the App Store or your browser. Go Back? does not record which rows you tap, and has nowhere to send that.

Exports

A spreadsheet you export is a file on your device. Where it goes next is up to you — Go Back? hands it to the system share sheet and takes no further part.

Deleting your data

Delete a place by swiping it, or delete everything under Settings → Delete All Data. That removes the data from this device and from your iCloud. No copy exists anywhere else.

Children

Go Back? collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat and no social features.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
