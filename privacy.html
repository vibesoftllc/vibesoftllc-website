Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Vibesoft LLC ("Vibesoft," "we," "our," or "us") is a limited liability company. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at vibesoftllc.com or use any application we publish. By using our website or applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

On This Website

This website is a static informational site. We do not operate analytics services, tracking pixels, behavioral advertising networks, or user account systems on this site. We do not collect any personal information through web forms.

If you contact us using a "mailto:" link, your email client will open a message addressed to us. Any message you choose to send will transmit your email address and message content to us.

In Our Applications

We do not collect your data. Nothing about you is sent to a Vibesoft server, because we do not operate one. Our applications have no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising identifiers.

The only circumstance in which any information leaves your device is when a feature you are using cannot work without it — a weather feature needs a location in order to return a forecast for it. In those cases we send only the minimum that feature requires, only to the party providing that function, only for as long as the request takes, and anonymised wherever anonymising it still lets the feature work.

Where an app offers such a feature, it is described at the point you switch it on, in that app’s own privacy notice, and on its App Store listing. Features of this kind are off until you enable them, unless enabling them discloses nothing about you at all — for example, an app asking a government website for a published tax figure sends nothing about anyone, so there is nothing to consent to.

Some of our applications can store a copy of your own data in your own iCloud account, at your instruction. That is your storage and your Apple Account. It is not collection, and we have no access to it.

Privacy Notices for Individual Apps

Each application has its own notice describing exactly what it does. Where an app sends anything anywhere, its notice names the recipient, what is sent, and whether the feature is on or off when you install it.

  • Nag Reminders — makes no network requests at all
  • FullPay: Video Poker Trainer — makes no network requests at all
  • Manila — makes no network requests at all; the Mac app has no network entitlement
  • MileClaim — three named exceptions, described individually

Third-Party Services

This website does not load content from third-party servers. All assets are served directly from vibesoftllc.com; no data is transmitted to any external party as a result of visiting this site.

Our applications do not integrate analytics, crash reporting, or advertising networks. Where an application uses a third party to perform a function you have asked for — a mapping provider to draw a map, a geocoder to turn a coordinate into a street address — only what that function requires is sent, and only when you use it. Those uses are named individually in the relevant app’s privacy notice, and that provider’s own privacy policy governs what they do with the request.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website does not set cookies or use tracking technologies of any kind.

Our applications use no tracking technologies either. They store your own settings and content on your device, using the operating system's ordinary local storage — that data stays on the device and is not transmitted to us. Nothing in our applications profiles you, follows you between apps, or builds a record of your behaviour.

How We Use Your Information

Information received through email contact on this website is used solely to respond to your inquiry, and for no other purpose.

We receive no information from our applications, so there is none for us to use. What an application stores stays on your device and is used only to provide the feature you are using it for — a mileage log calculates your deduction, a trainer scores your play. None of it is measured, reported, or aggregated by us.

We do not sell your personal information. We have never had any to sell.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. The reason is structural rather than a promise about our conduct: your data is on your device and not on any server of ours, so there is nothing for us to share, nothing for us to lose in a breach, and nothing we could be compelled to produce.

The one exception is email you send us directly. If we were required by law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process to produce correspondence, we would have that correspondence. We would not have anything from inside an application, because we never received it.

If Vibesoft were ever acquired or its assets sold, the successor would be bound by this policy. There would still be no user data to transfer.

Data Retention

Email correspondence is retained for as long as needed to address your inquiry and as required by applicable law.

We hold no data from our applications, so there is no retention period to describe. Data an application stores on your device is kept until you delete it or remove the app, and each app's notice explains how to erase it.

Children's Privacy

Our website and applications are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at dev@vibesoftllc.com and we will promptly delete it.

Your Privacy Rights

All users may contact us to ask what information we hold, request correction or deletion, or raise any privacy concern.

California residents have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information is collected about you
  • The right to delete personal information we hold
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dev@vibesoftllc.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website or applications after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
