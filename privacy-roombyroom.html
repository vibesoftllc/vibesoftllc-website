Room by Room

Privacy notice  ·  Effective November 2, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Room by Room collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but this app holds photographs of your valuables, their serial numbers and what they are worth, so here is exactly what happens to it.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline.

Where your inventory lives

On your device. If you have iCloud turned on, it also syncs to your own private iCloud database, the same way your Notes and Reminders do — Apple stores it in your account, under your Apple ID.

We operate no server. There is no Room by Room account, nothing to sign up for, and no email address collected. Your inventory is never sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Your photographs

Photographs stay on your device, in the app’s own storage, and sync through your iCloud with everything else. On iPhone and iPad they are encrypted at rest whenever the device is locked.

Nothing is uploaded to us, and nothing is analysed anywhere but on your device.

Serial numbers and barcodes

When you photograph a serial plate — or point the live scanner at it — the printed text and any barcode are read on your device by Apple’s Vision framework, which is part of the operating system. The photograph is not sent anywhere to be read, and neither is the text or the code.

A scanned barcode is only an identifier. Room by Room has no product database and looks nothing up: a scanned code is never sent anywhere, and it never fills in a product name.

Room by Room offers what it found and you choose whether to use it. It never fills the field for you.

What Room by Room does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store’s sense or any other.
  • No subscription, so there is no billing relationship and no renewal to lapse.

Network access

Room by Room makes no requests to any server of ours, because there is none. Two things use the network, and neither is your data going to us:

  • iCloud sync, between your own devices, through Apple.
  • Buying the unlock, through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Separately, More from Vibesoft (Settings › About) lists our other apps and websites. Tapping a row opens that app’s App Store page, or one of our websites, in the App Store or your browser. That is your device opening a page when you ask it to, not Room by Room sending anything: the app does not know which row you tapped, keeps no record of it, and has nowhere to send one. Apps that are not released yet are listed by name only, with nothing to open.

Warranty reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system, ahead of time. No push server is involved, and no reminder is sent from anywhere.

Exports

A PDF or CSV you export is a file on your device. Where it goes next is entirely up to you — Room by Room hands it to the system share sheet and takes no further part. If you email it to an insurance adjuster, their privacy practices are theirs, not ours.

Deleting everything

Settings → Delete All Data removes every item, room and photograph from this device and from your iCloud. Because there is no copy on a server of ours, that is the end of it. Export first if you might want it later.

Children

Room by Room collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves your devices.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
