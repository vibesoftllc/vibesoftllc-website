FullPay: Video Poker Trainer

Privacy notice  ·  Effective August 2, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

FullPay makes no network requests at all. There is no server, no account, and no analytics. The strategy engine runs entirely on your device, and the app works offline permanently.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category.

What FullPay stores

Two things, both in local storage on your device:

  • Your trainer statistics — accuracy per game, and the expected value your mistakes cost. The app has to remember this because scoring your play is the point of it.
  • Your preferences — which paytable you last played, sound and haptics settings, blackjack rule variant, whether to show the splash screen.

Neither leaves the device. There is no account to attach them to and nowhere to send them.

No wagering, no money, no gambling data

FullPay is a training tool. It simulates video poker in order to teach optimal play against real paytables. There is no real-money play, no wagering, no deposits, no withdrawals, and no link to any gambling operator. The credits in the app are a teaching device and have no value.

Because none of that exists, there is no financial or gambling activity to record, and none is recorded.

The app is rated 18+ for simulated gambling.

Purchases

FullPay is free to download, with Jacks or Better and the complete trainer free permanently. A single optional in-app purchase, FullPay Complete ($9.99, one time, no subscription), unlocks the other games plus the blackjack and keno modules. Family Sharing is enabled.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Entitlement is verified on the device by StoreKit — there is no receipt server, and no purchase information is reported to us.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

There are none. FullPay contains no analytics framework, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK of any kind. It requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your statistics and preferences from the device. There is nothing for us to delete on our end, because we never had it.

Children

FullPay is rated 18+ and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
