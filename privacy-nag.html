Nag Reminders

Privacy notice  ·  Effective August 2, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Nag makes no network requests at all. It has no server to talk to, no account, and no analytics. It works with the device in airplane mode, permanently, because it never needed a connection in the first place.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category.

What Nag does

Nag reads the reminders already on your device through EventKit, Apple's local framework, and re-notifies you about overdue ones until you mark them done. Everything — reading the list, deciding what is overdue, scheduling the alert, displaying it — happens on your device.

The notifications are local notifications, generated and delivered by your own device. There are no push notifications, because a push notification would require a server, and there is no server.

Where your information lives

  • Your reminders stay in Apple's Reminders app, where they already were. Nag reads and updates them in place. If you have iCloud enabled for Reminders, Apple syncs them as it always has — that is Apple's service and your Apple Account, not ours.
  • Your settings (schedule, quiet hours, which lists to watch, notification sound) are stored in local storage on the device.
  • Nagging history exists only in memory while the app is running and is not written to disk at all.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. We could not retrieve it if you asked us to.

Permissions

Reminders — required

Read and write access, so Nag can see which reminders are outstanding and mark one complete when you tap done. This is the entire function of the app. The data goes from EventKit to the screen and nowhere else.

Notifications — required

So the app can alert you. Local only.

Background refresh — iOS

So Nag can check for overdue reminders while it is closed. The check runs on your device and uses no network.

Nag does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access, and does not access HealthKit.

Purchases

Nag is free to download. A single optional in-app purchase, Nag Unlimited ($4.99, one time, no subscription), removes the cap on how many reminders are nagged.

The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. The app checks whether you own the unlock using StoreKit, which verifies it on the device — there is no receipt server and nothing is reported to us. The purchase covers both the iPhone and Mac versions.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

There are none. Nag contains no analytics framework, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is measured or reported, to us or to anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its settings from your device. Your reminders are unaffected — they belong to Apple's Reminders app and remain exactly as they were. There is nothing for us to delete on our end, because we never had anything.

Children

Nag collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no external links.
