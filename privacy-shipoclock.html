Ship O’Clock

Privacy notice  ·  Effective November 18, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Ship O’Clock collects nothing.

There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code that phones home. The app makes no network requests of its own.

This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline under Settings › About › Privacy Policy.

Where your data lives

Your voyages, port calls, packing lists and onboard spending are stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, they also sync through your own private CloudKit database — an area of your personal iCloud account that Vibesoft LLC cannot read, query, or access in any way.

There is no Vibesoft server holding your cruise history. That is not a promise about how we behave; it is a property of how the app is built. There is nothing to be shut down, sold, breached, or put behind a subscription with your records inside it.

What is stored on your device

  • Voyage details you enter: name, ship, dates, ports, times and time zones
  • Your packing list and what you have ticked off
  • Onboard charges you record
  • Your sound, haptic and budget preferences
  • A count of how many voyages you have created, in the device Keychain, so the one-time purchase can be honoured across a reinstall

Notifications

All-aboard alerts are scheduled locally with iOS and fire on your device. They are never sent from a server, because there is no server. Nothing about your itinerary leaves the device to produce them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. Vibesoft LLC never sees your payment details, and receives no personal information from the transaction.

Links to other apps

Settings includes a “More from Vibesoft” list of our other apps and websites. It is a fixed list built into the app, not downloaded. Tapping a row opens the App Store or your browser, exactly as tapping any link would; Ship O’Clock does not record which row you tapped and has nowhere to send it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published with a new version of the app.

Contact

Questions about this app: dev@vibesoftllc.com

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
