Manila

Privacy notice  ·  Effective August 29, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Manila collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but this app handles your documents — contracts, statements, things you redact and things you sign — so here is exactly what happens to them.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and its privacy manifest declares no collected data and no tracking. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, under Settings → Privacy, readable there offline.

No network access

Manila cannot reach the network. The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox without the network entitlement — neither com.apple.security.network.client nor com.apple.security.network.server — so the operating system refuses any outbound connection. This is enforced by macOS rather than promised by us, and anyone can verify it:

codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Manila.app

The only apparent exceptions open other apps, not a connection from Manila: clicking the support address opens your own email client, and the in-app purchase is handled by the App Store app on your Mac.

How Manila works

Every tool — merge, split, organize, compress, extract, images to PDF, redact, sign and verify — runs entirely on your Mac using Apple’s PDFKit. You choose which files Manila may read through the standard Open panel, and where results are written through the standard Save panel. Manila never sees any file you did not choose.

  • The PDFs and images you open stay wherever you keep them. Manila reads them in place and never copies them elsewhere.
  • Results — merged, split, redacted and signed PDFs, extracted text and images — go wherever you save them.
  • Your drawn or typed signature is kept as signature.png in the app’s container under Application Support, on this Mac only and never in iCloud. Delete the file, or replace the signature in the app, to remove it.
  • The signing key for tamper-evident receipts lives in the Secure Enclave on Macs that have one, otherwise in a key file in the same app container.
  • Operation history — which tool ran on which file name, when, and a hash of the output — is a history.jsonl file in the app container, on this Mac only. File → Export Operation History… lets you read it; deleting the container removes it.
  • The signer name you type for a signing certificate is stored in the app’s own preferences, in the app container.

Nothing in that list is transmitted anywhere. Manila’s app container is not synced to iCloud.

Signing receipts

When you turn on the audit trail for the Sign tool, Manila writes a .manila-receipt.json file next to your signed PDF. It contains the name you typed, the local date and time from your Mac’s clock, the document’s filename, hashes of the file before and after signing, and a signature made with a key that lives on your Mac. The receipt is a file you own; it goes only where you put it. It proves the file has not changed since signing — it is not an identity credential and no certificate authority is involved.

What Manila does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. No third-party service, font or library that communicates with a server is included.
  • No tracking, in the App Store’s sense or any other.
  • No subscription, so there is no billing relationship and no renewal to lapse.

Purchases

Manila is free to try and unlocked by a single one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Vibesoft LLC receives no personal information from it; Apple’s own privacy policy governs your Apple Account and payment details.

Your rights

Because Manila holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything the app produces is in files on your Mac under your control. We cannot access your data even if you ask us to — it is not stored anywhere we can reach.

Security

Your documents never leave your Mac, so the security of your files is the security of your Mac: FileVault, your login password, and your backups.

Children

Manila is a general-purpose utility and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves your Mac.

Changes to this policy

If Manila’s behaviour ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated in the app and on this page, with a new effective date, before that version ships.
