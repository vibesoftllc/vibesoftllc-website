ClearBid

Privacy notice  ·  Effective July 26, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

ClearBid collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but here is what it means specifically.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline.

What ClearBid stores, and where

Your clients, your price book, your estimates and any job-site photos you attach are stored on your device. If you have iCloud turned on, they also sync to your own private iCloud database — an area of your Apple account that we cannot read, list, or access in any way.

We operate no server for ClearBid. There is nowhere for your data to be sent, because there is nothing on the other end.

What that means if you stop paying

Nothing. ClearBid is a one-time purchase, and your estimates and price book are files on your own device. There is no subscription that can lapse and take your job history with it, and no export deadline.

What ClearBid does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no email is collected.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store's sense or any other.
  • No cost database, no rate lookups, and no sending your job details anywhere to be priced. Every number in a ClearBid quote came from you.

Network access

ClearBid works entirely offline. Pricing a job and generating a PDF never touch the network — which is the point, because job sites frequently have no signal.

Two things involve the network, and neither is ClearBid sending your data to us:

  • iCloud sync, if you have it on, moves your records between your own devices through your own Apple account. Apple handles it; we have no visibility into it.
  • Purchasing the unlock goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Separately, More from Vibesoft (Settings › About) lists our other apps. Tapping a row opens that app’s App Store page, or our website for CruiseSort, in the App Store or your browser. That is your device opening a page when you ask it to, not ClearBid making a request: the app itself still makes none, does not know which row you tapped, keeps no record of it, and has nowhere to send one.

Photos

Photos you attach to a quote are copied into ClearBid's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere by us, and ClearBid never writes back to your photo library.

Sending a quote

When you send a quote, you are sharing a PDF that ClearBid generated on your device, using whatever app you pick from the share sheet — mail, messages, or anything else. Where it goes from there is between you and that app.

Children

ClearBid collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves the device.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
