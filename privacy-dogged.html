Dogged

Privacy notice  ·  Effective November 20, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Dogged collects nothing. There is no account to create, no email to hand over, and no analytics in the app.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page says the same thing as the policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline under Settings › Privacy.

Where your habits live

Your habits, completions and streaks live in a file on your device. They are not uploaded to us, not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not analysed — anonymously or otherwise. The file goes wherever your device backup goes, and nowhere else.

We operate no server. Your habits are never sent to us, because there is nowhere for them to be sent.

Reminders

Every reminder and follow-up is scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is pushed to you from a server of ours, and nothing about when you complete a habit leaves your device to decide when the next reminder arrives.

Shared habits

Shared habits use Apple’s CloudKit sharing. When you share a habit, that habit and its completions travel between your iCloud account and the accounts of the people you invite, through Apple. They never pass through a server we operate, and we cannot read them. The people you share with can see the habit’s name, who has done it on which days, and the names iCloud shows for each participant. Only the habits you choose to share are shared.

What Dogged does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store’s sense or any other.
  • No subscription, so there is no billing relationship and no renewal to lapse.

Network access

Dogged makes no requests to any server of ours, because there is none. Two things use the network, and neither is your data going to us:

  • Shared habits, between iCloud accounts, through Apple, as described above.
  • Buying the unlock, through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Separately, More from Vibesoft (Settings › About) opens a page on this website in your browser. The app itself sends nothing and records nothing about that tap; what the browser sees is covered by the company policy.

Exporting your data

Settings › Your data › Export hands your whole history to the system share sheet as one file. Where it goes next is up to you; Dogged takes no further part. Export is never behind the purchase.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit from its row or its editor; that deletes every completion recorded for it. Stop sharing a habit from its detail screen. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device. No copy exists anywhere else, apart from what you shared, which lives in the iCloud accounts it was shared with.

Children

Dogged collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat and no public features; sharing works only by invitation between iCloud accounts.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
