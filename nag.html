Nag Reminders. It nags until you actually do it.

Apple Reminders notifies you once — then your task sits there, quietly forgotten. Nag watches your existing reminders and keeps notifying you, every 15 minutes or on your schedule, until the task is done. Complete it in Nag or in Reminders, and the nagging stops.

How it works

🔗

Uses your existing reminders

No new to-do system to adopt. Nag reads the Apple Reminders you already have and writes completions straight back — Siri, shared lists, and everything else keep working.

🔔

Persistent by design

One alert is easy to swipe away. Nag re-notifies on your schedule, escalating for overdue tasks and pacing by priority, until the task is actually complete.

😴

Respectful about it

Quiet hours silence the night. Snooze from the notification when now isn't the time, and cap the number of nags per task so persistence never becomes fatigue.

💻

Menu bar on the Mac

On macOS, Nag lives in the menu bar with your active reminders one click away — complete or snooze without leaving what you're doing.

Free to try. One purchase unlocks everything.

Nag is free and nags your top three reminders. A single $4.99 purchase — Nag Unlimited — removes the cap forever, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac together. No subscription, no account.

Private by architecture

Nag makes no network requests at all. Your reminders never leave your device, and there is nothing to sign up for. Read the Nag privacy notice or the Vibesoft privacy policy.
