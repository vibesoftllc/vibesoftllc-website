Deja

Privacy notice  ·  Effective July 26, 2026  ·  Vibesoft LLC

Deja collects nothing. That is the whole policy, but here is what it means specifically.

Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, in every category. This page is the same policy that ships inside the app, readable there offline.

What Deja does with your photos

Deja reads your photo library on your device to work out which pictures are duplicates of each other. It reduces each photo to a small numeric fingerprint — 64 bits, about the size of this sentence — and compares those fingerprints to find matches.

Your photos are never uploaded, copied off your device, or sent to us or to anyone else. We operate no server for Deja to send anything to.

What Deja stores

One file, on your device, containing those fingerprints and the identifiers Photos uses for your pictures. It exists so that scanning again is fast instead of starting from scratch. It contains no images. Deleting the app deletes it.

What Deja does not do

  • No analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no email is collected.
  • No advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking, in the App Store's sense or any other.

Network access

Deja makes no network requests. The Mac version ships without permission to open network connections at all, so this is enforced by macOS rather than promised by us.

Two things involve the network, and neither is Deja sending your data anywhere:

  • If you open the side-by-side compare view and a photo is stored in iCloud rather than on your device, Photos fetches it from your iCloud account for you to look at. During a scan Deja never does this — it skips those photos and tells you how many it skipped, rather than using your data allowance without asking.
  • Purchasing the unlock goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Deleting photos

Deja only deletes photos you have selected and confirmed. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Children

Deja collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no data leaves the device.

The company policy

This notice covers one application. The company-wide policy, which also covers this website and how to reach us about a privacy question, is at vibesoftllc.com/privacy.
